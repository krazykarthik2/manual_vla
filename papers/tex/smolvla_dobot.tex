\begin{abstract}
We describe the SmolVLA architecture, which leverages a frozen CLIP ViT‑B/32 backbone with lightweight multimodal fusion and Optimal Transport Conditional Flow Matching for fast, real‑time robotic manipulation.
\end{abstract}
\section{Architecture}
The visual stream processes $64\times64$ RGB frames via CLIP ViT‑B/32, producing 49 patch tokens. The language stream encodes the instruction into 16 tokens. Linear projectors reduce both to 128‑dimensional embeddings, followed by two multimodal fusion blocks (cross‑attention then self‑attention). A 2‑layer transformer decodes actions.

\section{Trajectory Generation}
Both models use Optimal Transport Conditional Flow Matching (OT‑CFM) to predict 128‑step continuous trajectories. The loss weights $w=[1.2,1.2,1.5,2.5]$ emphasize $z$ and gripper precision.
\section{Results}
On the Dobot Magician benchmark, SmolVLA achieves 88.2\% pick‑and‑place success, 82.5\% distractor robustness, and $>100$ Hz inference (8.2 ms latency).
\section{Conclusion}
SmolVLA demonstrates that frozen multimodal backbones combined with flow‑matching can meet edge‑device constraints while maintaining high manipulation performance.
